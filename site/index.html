Remember when friends just happened? Same team, same class, same corner café. Issho brings that back. Tell us what you love. We’ll find a few people nearby who love it too, and a time and place to do it together.

It starts with a plan

Pick something you’d do anyway.

Photo walks, bouldering, ramen crawls, board-game cafés. Tag each activity Love it, Like it or Open to try, and Plans shows what’s on near you, best matches first. The activity breaks the ice, so you don’t have to.

Sign up for any that grab you and tell us when you’re free. No date yet, no pressure.

Date set

Enough of you said yes. It’s on.

When enough people can make the same time, we lock in a date and split everyone into small groups, matched on what you love, a language you share and the group size you like.

Going

A few people, not a crowd.

Your invite lands in Together, under “Needs you”. Confirm your place and see who’s coming. Photos are only shared with your group. No profiles to swipe, no one-to-one messages: Issho is for making friends.

On the day

Show up. The activity does the talking.

The group chat opens ahead of time, so you can say hi and spot each other at the gates. Tap “I’m here” when you arrive, and share the outing with someone you trust.

How was it?

Pick who you’d meet again. Privately.

Nobody sees your picks. Pick each other, and you’re more likely to share a group again. Anyone you’d rather not see again, you won’t.

A crew

Strangers on Saturday, regulars by spring.

Clicked with your group? We’ll offer you a crew: your own group chat, with a fresh plan picked for you every week or so. It starts once at least three of you say yes, and you can leave any time.

Find your people through the things you already love.

Issho is a new way to build community, rooted in the way we always have. We bring small groups of like-minded people together for real-world activities, so friendships grow from time spent doing what you enjoy.

One thread, one sun.

Issho (一緒) means “together”.The character is self-explanatory.

  • 一“one”

    Togetherness. It all starts with being one.

  • 糸“thread”

    Connection. The thread that ties us to each other.

  • 土 ノpart of 者, “person”

    People are the most important thing, so they are the ground everything else sits on.

  • 日“sun”

    We are all under the same sky.

Community, without the awkward part.

Making friends as an adult is hard because no one is organising it. Issho does the organising: tell us what you enjoy and when you are free, and we propose a concrete plan with a small group of nearby people who share your interests.

  1. Pick what you enjoyPhoto walks, board games, climbing, ramen crawls. Start from activities you already like.
  2. Meet a small groupWe match you with a few nearby people and suggest a time and a place that works for everyone.
  3. Keep the ones you click withAfterwards, privately choose who you would like to meet again. Matches become a regular crew.

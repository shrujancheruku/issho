# outings

Owns outings and their seats: the group matching formed on a date for a plan, and each person's place in it ([Outings](../../../docs/spec/outings.md), [Matching](../../../docs/spec/matching.md)). The tables are `app.outings` and `app.seats` (`db/migrations/00007_outings.sql`); no other package reads them. `plans` and `outings` load matching's inputs and store its results ([Architecture and stack](../../../docs/spec/architecture.md#the-seams)): `outings` reads a round's signups from `plans.Signups.Forming`, the languages, gender and "only women and non-binary people" setting of each person from `people.Service.Profile`, and calls the pure function in [`matching`](../matching/README.md).

It **forms outings and makes their invitations**, takes each invited person's **yes or no for 48 hours**, **sets the outing at the third yes**, records an **unanswered** seat when the 48 hours end, and lets a person **going cancel**. Check-in and feedback are later tickets'. The owner's approval of a *proposed* outing, the extra invitations and the seat order come with the outing in full (milestone 4), so an outing is formed **offered** (its invitations are out) and each person in a group gets one invited seat.

## Using it

| Function | What |
|---|---|
| `New(clock, pool, signups, people)` | The service |
| `Service.Form(ctx, plan)` | Groups the signups to the plan's running round and stores each group as an outing with a seat for each person, **in one transaction**. Returns how many outings it made. Forming again makes none for anyone who already has a seat in the round, so running it twice for the same signups makes no second outing. A plan that isn't there or isn't shown is `plans.ErrNotFound` |
| `Service.Mine(ctx, account)` | The account's own seats, earliest date first: `Seat` has the outing, the plan, the date, the part of the day, the plan's start time (`HH:MM`, the city's clock), the languages the group shares, the state and when the invitation went out and ends. It takes the account and holds no other person, so it can't return an outing the account has no seat in, and a name is never in it |
| `Service.Answer(ctx, account, outing, Yes or No)` | Records the account's answer to its own invitation and returns the seat as `Mine` shows it. Open for the 48 hours from the invitation; a no can be changed to a yes inside them. A yes is held (`SeatAccepted`) until `policy.YesesToSet` (3) have said yes: the third sets the outing, makes everyone who said yes going and takes that time off their other signups, **in one transaction**, and a yes after that is going at once. `ErrNotFound` for an outing that isn't there and for one the account has no seat in alike; `ErrInvitationClosed` after the 48 hours, for an outing not set and for a no from someone going; `ErrTimeTaken` for a yes while the account is going to another outing at the same date and part of the day; `ErrInvalid` for any other answer. A yes from someone already going changes nothing |
| `Service.Cancel(ctx, account, outing)` | A person going gives their seat up: it is recorded as `SeatCancelled`. `ErrNotGoing` for anyone else, `ErrNotFound` as above |
| `Service.Expire(ctx, outing)` | Records the unanswered seats of an outing whose 48 hours have run (`SeatUnanswered`) and, if it is still offered when its last invitation has ended, marks it `OutingNotSet`; those who said yes stay accepted (left waiting). It closes only what is due, so running it early or twice changes nothing |
| `FormArgs`, `NewFormWorker(svc)`, `AddWorker(workers, svc)` | The River job `outings_form` that calls `Form` for a plan. Its arguments are a plan's id and nothing about a person. `cmd/worker` registers it. A plan that isn't there or isn't shown finishes the job without an error (retrying can't change it) |
| `ExpireArgs`, `NewExpireWorker(svc)` | The River job `outings_expire_invitations` that calls `Expire` for an outing. `Form` inserts it **in the transaction that makes the invitations**, scheduled for the end of their 48 hours, so it exists if they do and not otherwise. Its argument is an outing's id. `AddWorker` registers it too |

**Invitations.** Each seat starts `invited`, with `invited_at` (the moment the group is formed: nothing is delivered yet, so that is when the invitation goes out) and `invitation_ends_at`, `policy.InvitationWindow` (a fixed 48 hours) later. An outing is formed `offered`. The date is never less than 3 days after the invitations go out: forming reads only the ticks that are still `policy.MinTickLeadDays` ahead now ([`plans`](../plans/README.md)).

**What a seat holder is shown** is the date, the time (the part of the day the group ticked and the plan's start), every language the group shares, where the outing stands and how many have said yes, which is a **count, never who**. Someone **going** also sees the **first names of the others going** (`Seat.GoingFirstNames`, read through `people.Service.FirstNames`, never its tables): not their own, and not a surname, for anyone going and for nobody else. The RPCs are `OutingService` (`internal/rpc/outing.go`): `ListMyOutings` takes no account and no outing id; `AnswerInvitation` and `CancelSeat` name an outing but act only on the caller's own seat in it, and an outing the caller has no seat in is not found, as one that doesn't exist is.

**The states.** A seat is `invited` until answered. A yes while the outing is not set is `accepted` (a state the spec has no word for: the yes has to wait for the third, and it still counts as a yes); setting the outing makes every accepted seat `going`. A no is `declined`; a seat still invited when its 48 hours end is `unanswered`; a person going who cancels is `cancelled`. An outing is `offered` until the third yes (`set`), or until its last invitation's 48 hours end with fewer (`not_set`: those who said yes stay `accepted`, which is "left waiting"). Answering takes a lock on the outing's row to the end of its transaction, so three people saying yes at once are counted one after the other and the third sets it once.

**Nobody is double-booked** (docs/spec/matching.md). Forming runs per plan, so a person can hold an invitation to two outings at one date and part of the day and say yes to both. The first to be set makes them going; in that same transaction their other seats at that time that are still `invited` or `accepted` become `declined` (each of those outings' yes counts follows, as it is counted from the seats), and a seat going is never touched this way. From then on a yes to another outing at that time is `ErrTimeTaken`. To make that safe, a yes first takes an advisory lock on each person it may make going (the caller, and the accepted ones when the outing isn't set), keyed on the account, the date and the part of the day, in account order and after the outing's own row, then reads the seat again. Two third yeses on outings that share a person queue on that lock, so one sets its outing and the other's yes finds the person gone, and neither waits on the other (no deadlock).

**Going is the one commitment, and it takes the time.** When a person becomes going, `plans.Signups.ReleaseTime` takes that date and part of the day off their other signups (the outing's own round is kept), inside the same transaction. A yes that only waits, or a no, takes nothing.

**Not done yet** (later tickets): the 24-hour mark, so every cancel is `cancelled` and none is a `late_cancel`, and cancelling after the start isn't refused; the outing with too few going (cancelled when only 1 is, set outing goes ahead with 2); the seat order and a seat given up being offered again; notifications. See the resolution of #624.

**Two runs at once, and twice.** A run takes an advisory lock on the round, held to the end of its transaction, then reads who already has a seat; so River delivering the job twice, or two jobs at once, forms the round once. The database backs it: `app.seats` is unique on `(round_id, account_id)`, since nobody is in two groups of one round, so a run that did race fails rather than double-seats. A person who signs up after a run is formed on the next one, among the others who have no seat.

**What matching reads.** A signup's ticks, and the person's languages, gender and setting. Kept-apart pairs are an empty input until safety exists (milestone 6) and the city's closed dates are empty until the city calendar exists ([The build order](../../../docs/spec/build-order.md)). A person with no profile is left out (a signup needs one, so there is none). `people.Service.Profile` is read once for each person in a round; a round is tens of people, so there is no batch read.

**The 48 hours** count from `invited_at`, which is the moment of forming until delivery exists. They are `policy.InvitationWindow`, and the 3 yeses `policy.YesesToSet`; both are constants.

**When it runs.** Nothing enqueues the job yet: rounds on the real clock are milestone 4's, and the end-to-end test's clock RPC (#622) enqueues `FormArgs` itself. A caller enqueues it with `client.InsertTx(ctx, tx, outings.FormArgs{PlanID: id}, nil)`, in the transaction that causes it.

## Retention

Both tables have a [registry](../retention/registry.json) entry: `app.outings` holds no one's data (the people are in `app.seats`) and `app.seats` is kept while the account exists ([Privacy](../../../docs/spec/privacy.md)).

## Tests

On the throwaway Postgres (`internal/testdb`), through the exported API, with a fake clock, the real `plans`, `people` and `matching`, on the fixture city.

| File | Proves |
|---|---|
| `outings_test.go` | Forming makes one outing with an invited seat for each person, with the date, the time, the shared languages and the time its 48 hours end; forming twice, or at the same time, makes no second outing; people who signed up later are formed on the next run, among themselves; matching's rules hold through the job (a shared language, the women-and-non-binary-only setting); a date less than 3 days after the invitations is never set; a run that fails part-way leaves no outing; the database holds one signup per person per round and a person who changed their times is one entry, the latest; a person with no seat is never shown an outing |
| `job_test.go` | The River job forms a plan's groups once however often it is delivered, and finishes for a plan that isn't there |
| `answer_test.go` | An invited person says yes or no to the last second of the 48 hours and not after, a no becomes a yes inside them, a person with no seat finds no outing, the third yes sets the outing and makes those who said yes going (also three at once), a later yes is going at once, a seat unanswered at 48 hours is recorded by the job made with the invitation (scheduled for then, safe early and twice), an outing with fewer than 3 is not set and takes no more answers, the count of yeses and the first names going, cancelling, and the time taken from the other signups of those who are going; nobody is double-booked (the other open seat at that time becomes a no and its count follows, a yes at a time already taken is refused, two third yeses at once on outings that share a person set one and don't deadlock) |

# Prototype: good plans in Da Nang–Hoi An

Issue [#542](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/542), part of the v2 map [#532](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/532). Built and judged 2026-11-18. Throwaway: this branch is never merged.

The interactive page the owner judged is a private artifact: https://claude.ai/artifact/94AEHTxiwMHkp3rUm9qnG2. This file is the durable record. Google ratings and review counts are left out on purpose (see [#537](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/537)).

## Question

Shown side by side, which way of making a plan produces ones the owner would actually go on?

## Method

Three briefs, each made three ways by hand, shown blind. The owner answered "would you go?" and "send a friend, 1–5" for each, then saw how each was made. Every place was confirmed to exist, with opening hours, on Google Places. Costs were estimates unless noted.

- **Google alone:** the first results for a plain query, chosen by popularity, roughly as v1 chose.
- **Local sources:** Vietnamese press (VnExpress, Tuổi Trẻ, Tiền Phong, Báo Văn Hoá), the Michelin Guide Da Nang 2026 list on the city tourism portal, Vietnamese guides, one Vietnamese-language blog. Google only for existence and hours.
- **Vetted pieces:** a meeting-point piece, a stretch piece, a food piece and fact pieces, composed. Nobody on the ground vetted them; several rested on Google alone.
- **One local person's recommendation:** not tested. No named local writing in Vietnamese with plan-ready detail could be found by search, and the owner chose to close without supplying picks.

Mean "send a friend": local sources 4.3 (3 of 3 "would go"), vetted pieces 2.7 (1 of 3), Google alone 2.3 (1 of 3).

The owner's notes, in short:

- Plans 2 and 7 (Google): tourist traps. The night market is "full of drunk backpackers".
- Plans 1 and 5 (pieces): a meal and a walk on a busy road, or a coffee and a short beach walk. Nothing a person couldn't arrange alone.
- Plan 3: good food, but an alley mouth is a poor meeting point and the walk has no appeal of its own.
- Plans 4, 6, 8, 9: good starts, all too short; walks need marked things of interest.
- Overall: "The plans were too short to be compelling… If someone is signing up to a plan, I think they expect more than just the plan they would make to meet a friend for a coffee… people bond by doing things they like together."

## The rule change

After round one the owner changed "worth the trip": 3–4 hours around at least one thing the group does together that isn't eating or looking; food supports it; interesting things along a walk are marked as options (not a hard rule); a paid, bookable activity is allowed when its cost is stated up front.

## Round two (not blind, local sources, activity at the centre)

| # | Plan | Go | Friend | Owner's note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | Bánh xèo Bà Dưỡng, walk up Hoàng Diệu, a pottery session at a studio on Nguyễn Chí Thanh | Yes | 5 | Put the activity before the meal: it breaks the ice and gives the meal a topic |
| 11 | Mân Thái fish market at dawn, a sunrise paddle on stand-up boards, Mì Quảng Nia, optional coffee | Yes | 5 | Local market, an interesting and accessible activity, a good meal to finish |
| 12 | A lantern-making class, xí mà, walk to Cẩm Nam for bánh đập and hến, optional bài chòi | No | 4 | The lantern class is for tourists; people who live here won't care for it. The food stops stay good |

Owner's closing point: activities will need more human vetting than the supporting plan around them; good places to eat, drink and walk in an area are easier to find than activities that are compelling for locals.

## What the hand-run accuracy checks showed

- **Cost** failed on almost every plan: Google returned no prices, and only two prices came from a source (a dinner in VnExpress, a board rental in Vietbao).
- **Group fit** was rarely confirmable from any source (bookings, seating at stalls).
- **Hours**: Google listed the Hội An night market as open 09:00–22:00. The best food stops sell until they run out.
- **Season and weather** (monsoon, sunrise time, lantern night, floods) came only from press and earlier research, never from Google.
- **Meeting points**: a stretch of beach and an alley mouth both failed the owner's eye even where a check would pass.

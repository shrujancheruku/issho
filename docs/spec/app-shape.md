# The app's shape

From [The app's shape](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/560), amended by [Finding leads on a schedule](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/568) and [The v2 glossary and domain model](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/569). The four layouts the owner judged are in the [record](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/blob/prototype/the-apps-shape/docs/prototypes/the-apps-shape.md) on the unmerged `prototype/the-apps-shape` branch.

The visual identity stands from v1. The combination below was agreed from four rough layouts and was not itself drawn; individual screens, the first-run flow and the screens the site's story borrows are not yet specified.

## Tabs

**Together**, **Plans**, **You**. The owner and insiders get a fourth, **Making**; members never see it.

## What opens first

Plans when the person has nothing in play (including a visitor with no account); otherwise Together.

## Together

Everything of the person's.

- A **"Needs you"** block sits on top: each thing that needs an answer is a card answered in place (an invitation, an offered seat, "still going?", "keep waiting?", a crew offer or proposal).
- Below it, everything else is **ordered by time, not by type**: outings on their date, the day a signup will hear, the day feedback closes.
- The tab carries a count of what needs an answer. This is how the app works with no inbox.
- **Crews** are part of Together, with no tab of their own. A crew's screen opens on its room with any live proposal pinned.

## Plans

The city's list, the same for a visitor and a member, with the three filters above it: area, "up to" a price, and activity type ([People](people.md#finding-plans)).

The **activity types** are Make, Move, Play, Walk, Learn and Watch.

## The outing is the plan's page plus a private strip

- The strip holds the date and time, the languages the group shares, the group (with "you met at" and "runs Issho" markers), the stage, and check-in.
- Below it is the plan exactly as anyone sees it. A plan is never drawn on a second screen, and a plan the person has signed up to shows its state ("you signed up, hear by") on the same page.
- **Chat is a pane within that screen**, and within a crew's screen. There is no list of chats.
- "Propose to a crew" is on every plan's page.

## You

The profile, join links, the city and display language, the "only women and non-binary people" setting, the one nudge switch and the room mutes, "Suggest a place or an activity", "Contact Issho", the code of conduct and "delete my account".

## Making

The one plan editor, vouching, re-checks due and doubted pieces, the maker's plans and pieces, and the city's leads list, where the owner and insiders take, dismiss or vouch a lead and run the lead search by hand.

## Accepted as drawn

- **A signup** is a sheet over the plan: a grid of days and parts of the day, filled in from last time. The profile questions come before it the first time only.
- **Push permission** is asked on the screen that confirms the first signup.
- **States are one line** at the top of Together and of the plans list, never a page: waitlisted, a city on a break, paused, and the "get the app" line in a phone browser. "No group formed, keep waiting?" is a "Needs you" card.
- **In a browser** a visitor sees the plans in columns with "Sign in" and "Join the waitlist" and no tabs; signed in, the top bar carries the same sections. A plan's page is the whole plan and one button. A waitlisted person sees the plans and the one status line.

## Rejected

- **No tabs:** crews had no home but a sheet, and a dock hid all but the next thing.
- **A Crews tab:** empty for most people for weeks.
- **A messenger, where the room is the outing:** no room exists before an outing is set, so the loop would split across two tabs; it also puts talking, not going out, at the centre.

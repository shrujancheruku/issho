# Matching

From [Matching](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/549), amended by [The outing](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/550) and [Languages](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/556), with [Running Issho](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/557).

## What matching is

Matching forms groups and sets dates among the signups to a city's plans. It suggests no plans. "Matching forms a group" means it creates an outing and sends its invitations.

It never reads: home area, travel time, tastes, budget, energy, age, display language, ratings, or one-sided picks. A signup to a plan that names its meeting point and price is the person's consent to the distance, the cost and the activity.

There are no scored weights. Rules and the seat order decide, so a decision can be explained to someone who asks why they weren't picked.

## Rules that are never broken

- A group is 3 to 6 people.
- Kept-apart pairs are never grouped.
- **The language belongs to the group, not the plan.** A group can form in any language all of its people share; matching requires one shared spoken language and prefers no language over another. It never builds a two-language group. The invitation names every shared language ("this group can speak Vietnamese and English"). No plan is greyed out for anyone, and the same plan can run as a Vietnamese-speaking group one week and an English-speaking group the next.
- **Only women and non-binary people.** A person who gave their gender as woman or non-binary can turn this on; every group they join is then made up only of women and non-binary people. The others in the group don't need the setting on. They are told it means a longer wait. Gender is self-declared. There is no men-only setting.
- Nobody is double-booked: an outing a person is going to blocks that time in their other signups.
- No outing is set on a closed date in the city's calendar; a caution date needs the owner's okay.

## Preferences

Followed whenever a hard rule and the seat order allow:

- Nobody is the only woman or the only man. Non-binary people and people who gave no gender are exempt.
- **Mutual picks together.** Two people who picked each other, signed up to the same plan with a shared time, are put in the same group.
- **No lone stranger.** A group is never one stranger plus people who all know each other, unless the choice is that or no outing for the stranger.
- Sizes nearest 4 or 5.

## The seat order

When more people fit a group than it has seats:

1. People left waiting, most rounds first
2. People who have never had an outing
3. Mutual picks kept together
4. Everyone else, earliest signup first
5. People who already have an outing set in the current week
6. People with a late cancel or a final no-show against them ([Safety](safety.md#late-cancels-and-no-shows))

The order decides seats only and never bends a hard rule. There is no cap on outings per week; a person's third outing just never takes someone's first.

When two groups need the same person at the same time, the person goes where fewer people end up without an outing; a tie goes to the plan whose people have waited longest.

## Rounds

- Each open plan runs in **rounds** of a fixed 7 days. The hear-by date is the round's last day, the same for everyone signed up.
- **Times.** At signup the person ticks parts of the day (morning, afternoon, evening, in the city's clock hours) on real dates, from 3 days ahead to 7 days after the round's last day. Only the parts of the day the plan's pieces allow are offered, and the ticks are prefilled from the person's last signup.
- **Forming.** A group is formed early as soon as 5 people who can be grouped share a time, and on the last day from any 3 or more. The round stays open to its last day for everyone else, and forms as many groups as its signups allow, each with its own date, within any limit the maker set as a condition.
- **Sizes.** Matching first seats the most people, then prefers sizes nearest 4 or 5, then fewer groups: 6 people make one group of 6, 7 make 4 and 3, 8 make 4 and 4, 9 make 5 and 4. The date that seats the most people wins; a tie goes to the earliest.
- **Invitations.** An outing's date is never less than 3 days after its invitations go out. Matching invites up to two more than it needs; seats go in the order people say yes, up to 6, and anyone who says yes after that is told the group is full and counts as left waiting.
- **What "hear by" promises.** By that date the person has an invitation or a "no group this round". The outing is set within the invitation's 48 hours, once at least 3 have said yes. With fewer than 3 yeses it is not set, and those who said yes count as left waiting.
- **Empty seats.** A set outing keeps its empty seats open, up to the plan's 6, until the 24-hour mark. Issho offers them in seat order to people signed up to the plan who ticked that time and fit the group, including people who sign up after the outing was set ([Outings](outings.md#seats-stay-open-until-the-24-hour-mark)).

## Too few people

A round that ends with no group for a person leaves them **left waiting**. The plan's next round opens at once, and each person left waiting is asked "keep waiting for the next round?" in one tap; with no answer the signup lapses. They are told, not moved to another plan.

## How many plans are open

The number of open plans is capped at one per 6 active people, never fewer than 4 (10 of 12 at the Pilot's 60 people, 5 at 30). Plans over the cap stay visible as "opens again soon" with a "tell me" button and take turns: plans with people waiting first, then the better record. The ratio is one worldwide number, revised from Pilot data.

## The owner approves groups in the Pilot

In the Pilot an outing is **proposed** until the owner approves it; nobody has been told. The owner can approve, remove a person, move a person to another proposed outing of the same plan, or reject. Every edit is checked against the rules above and needs a reason. The owner cannot change the date or the meeting point. With no act in 24 hours the group is invited as proposed.

Approval turns off once the owner has let 20 groups in a row through unchanged, and it is a Pilot-only step that does not return in a later city.

## The got-an-outing share

Computed per week, Monday to Sunday in the city's time:

- **Counted in:** people with at least one signup in a round that closed that week, including carried-over signups, minus anyone who was invited and declined.
- **Got an outing:** those going on a set outing from one of those rounds.

Crew outings don't count. An outing Issho later cancels still counts here and is tracked in the separate "went ahead" number.

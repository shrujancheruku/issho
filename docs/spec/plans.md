# Plans

From [Plans people make](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/541), refined by [Building blocks](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/544), [Languages](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/556) and [The v2 glossary and domain model](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/569).

## Who makes plans

Only **insiders** and Issho. Ordinary members never write a plan. An insider's plan goes round the same loop and faces the same bar and review as any other ([The core loop](core-loop.md), [The plan bar](plan-bar.md)).

## Suggestions

- Anyone with an account can send a **suggestion**: one place or one activity, with a line on why it is good and when. Never a whole plan. Having attended an outing is not required. The person accepts the code of conduct first ([People](people.md)).
- The person must say if they have a stake in the place (owner, staff, family). This protects "venues never pay for placement".
- Nothing is promised: a suggestion may never be used. It joins the city's leads list ([Leads](leads.md)) and reaches a plan only through an insider or the owner vouching for it, and then through review.
- When a suggestion first appears in a plan, the person who sent it gets a private notice with a link. There is no public credit on the plan.

## A plan runs many times

Every plan is standing; there is no separate "repeat" act. A plan that ran, wasn't rated poorly and still passes its accuracy checks opens a new round on its own. It shows its **record**: how many times it has run and its "send a friend" score, with "last confirmed by people who went on ⟨date⟩" beside it ([Plan knowledge](plan-knowledge.md)). A person repeats a plan only by signing up again or sharing it. The plans that worked are the asset.

## A plan's states

Draft, in review, open, closed or retired.

- **Open**: taking signups in a round.
- **Closed** always has a reason: between rounds, failed a check, a doubted piece, rated poorly, or its maker suspended. People see "opens again soon" for every reason.
- **Retired**: dropped for good and kept on record with the reason.

## What a maker sets

- **Content:** title, description, the stops and their order, time at each, the legs between them, notes, what to bring.
- **Conditions:** the days of the week, parts of the day and months it runs in, and a group size within 3 to 6. A plan's cost is the sum of its pieces' costs, and it can run only when all its pieces are open; a maker can narrow these and never widen them ([Pieces](pieces.md)).
- **Never:** the date, the people, the languages, the fee, or whether they attend.

Everything that must be true on the day (times, price, address, meeting point, each leg's mode and minutes) is a field, not prose. The maker writes the prose in any one of the city's languages ([Languages](languages.md)).

## Credit

An insider's plan shows their first name and a line about them. Issho's own plans show no maker.

## Changes

- The maker edits their own plan between rounds. Every edit re-runs the accuracy checks; a change to the stops or their order goes back through review, and the prose is translated again. An edited plan awaiting review keeps running unedited.
- While people are signed up or going, only accuracy fixes (a like-for-like swap), and those people are told.
- Issho can edit or withdraw any plan, and tells the maker.
- The record survives small changes. Replacing the activity, or most of the stops, makes a new plan with no record.

## A plan that goes stale

A plan that fails its checks between rounds goes to its maker to fix and stays closed meanwhile. Unfixed after 14 days, Issho fixes it or retires it. A plan in Issho's care that isn't fixed in 14 days is retired, not left closed.

## When the maker leaves

- **Leaves, or stops being an insider:** the plans stay and keep running, in Issho's care or another insider's. The name stays unless they ask for it to be removed.
- **Deletes their account:** the name goes, the plans stay.
- **Suspended for conduct:** the name comes off at once and the plans are closed until the owner has reviewed each one; then they run without a maker's name, or are retired.
- **Rights:** by making a plan, an insider lets Issho keep using and changing it. Suggestions and plans are licensed to Issho for good ([Privacy](privacy.md)).

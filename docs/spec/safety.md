# Safety

From [Safety](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/553), amended by [Privacy and legal obligations in the product](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/559), with [Running Issho](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/557).

## Reports

- A person can report anyone they shared a set outing, a crew or a room with, including someone who cancelled after the room opened.
- A report starts from the person's name, a message or feedback.
- The window is while the room exists (about 31 days after the plan's end for an outing).
- Reasons: unwanted romantic or sexual attention; harassment or abuse; felt unsafe; seems under 18; something else.
- A second report about the same person adds to the first.
- At most 5 reports an hour. A knowingly false report is a conduct breach.

## Kept apart

- One concept, reached three ways: a report, a "rather not" in feedback, or a button on the person's name at any time, labelled "Don't group me with them again".
- Private, for good, no undo, no moderator.
- Two people kept apart are never grouped or offered a crew together again. When two people going on the same outing, or two crew members, become kept apart, the reported or not-wanted person is removed ([Outings](outings.md), [Crews](crews.md)).
- It does not hide the other person's messages in a room the two still share.
- "Block" is used only in store-review notes, never in the product.

## What a report does at once

1. The pair is kept apart, with the removals above.
2. The moderator is alerted.
3. For "felt unsafe" and "harassment or abuse" only, the reported person goes on **hold**: no new group or invitation until a moderator has looked.

- Outings already set are left for the moderator to judge.
- A hold is invisible to the person and is not a standing.
- A hold lapses after 72 hours if no moderator has looked, and the lapse raises an alert.
- A single report never suspends anyone by itself.

## The moderator

- Four outcomes: dismiss, **warning**, suspension, lifting a suspension.
- A warning is a written notice naming the rule, kept on record.
- A suspension is open-ended until a moderator lifts it, never timed. A suspended person can sign in only to read the notice, appeal, export or delete.
- First look within 24 hours, and before the start of any set outing the reported person is going to.
- The owner is the only moderator in the Pilot. Insiders never moderate. A second named moderator is required before the soft launch ([Running Issho](running-issho.md#the-second-moderator)).
- A moderator suspends on a "seems under 18" report.

## Late cancels and no-shows

- **The first is forgiven.** A person's first late cancel or final no-show in a rolling 90 days counts nothing and costs nothing.
- After that, over the same 90 days, a late cancel counts 1 and a final no-show counts 2.
- Any count: back of the seat order for the person's next group.
- 3 points: paused 14 days. 3 points again within 90 days of returning: paused 30 days.
- No excuse forms; a moderator can waive one. Saying "running late" in the room changes nothing.
- A pause never becomes a suspension. A paused person keeps their crews, rooms and any outing already set.

## What people are told

| Who | What |
|---|---|
| The reporter, at once | Received; you won't be grouped with them again; we look within 24 hours |
| The reporter, on closing | Reviewed, and the action we judged right was taken. Never the outcome |
| The person reported | Nothing for a report, a dismissal or a hold |
| The person warned or suspended | In the app and by email: the rule broken and what follows. Never the reporter, the outing or the date |
| A person removed because of kept apart | No reason |

## Appeals

- One appeal per warning or suspension, by replying to the email within 30 days. Answered within 7 days; the answer is final.
- In the Pilot the owner hears it. From the soft launch each moderator hears appeals against the other's decisions.

## Flags

A flag shows a person to a moderator and does nothing else.

| Flag | Rule |
|---|---|
| Not wanted | Kept apart by 3 different people across at least 2 outings |
| Gender-lean picks | 6 or more picks, 80% or more to one gender, against who they met |
| Crew exit | A crew member leaves a crew giving "made me uncomfortable" |
| Second warning | A report about someone who already has a warning goes to the top of the queue |
| Keeps people apart a lot | Kept apart from 5 people, or their acts removed someone from a set outing or crew twice in 90 days |

The removal rule stands even though it can be misused to push someone out: safety outweighs the misuse, and the last flag catches it.

## Verification and declarations

- No identity verification before the public launch; the chain of join links and the waitlist are the check. A verified phone number is added at the soft launch ([Privacy](privacy.md)).
- One account per person. A second account, or a false age, gender or residence declaration, is a conduct breach a moderator can suspend for.
- Evading a suspension with a new email: suspended on discovery.
- No place is screened by name: every place a plan names is a piece someone vouched for.

## Join links

- Nothing automatic. When someone is suspended the moderator sees who brought them in and the other people that member brought in.
- A member who brought in 2 people now suspended loses their remaining join links, by the moderator's hand, and is told why.
- Giving someone a join link is never itself a conduct breach.

## Emergencies

- Issho is not an emergency service and says so.
- The on-the-day view shows the city's emergency numbers (a city setting) and a "report now" action that alerts the moderator at once.
- No live location sharing, panic button or monitoring of outings.
- The staff side is the safety runbook in [Running Issho](running-issho.md#the-safety-runbook).

## Deleting an account

- Erased: the account, the profile, signups and the picks the person made. Anything set counts as a cancel with no points. Messages show "someone who left".
- Kept against a keyed hash of the email (the **tombstone**): any suspension or warning; reports about them, with an open report still reviewed; kept-apart pairs where they were the one reported or not wanted. A later account with that email inherits these.
- Reports they made stay, without their name.
- Each part of the tombstone has a lifetime, in [Privacy](privacy.md#the-tombstone).

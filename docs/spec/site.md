# The site

From [The site](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/558), amended by [The v2 glossary and domain model](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/569).

## What the site is

- **Issho works whole in a browser at every stage**, with no limits, chat and check-in included. The phone app is the recommended way because only it gets push. A signed-in person in a phone browser sees one dismissible line, "Get the app so you don't miss your invitation", and nothing else nags.
- **One address to a visitor.** `joinissho.com` holds the front page, the city's plans, each plan's page, sign-in and the product.
- **Search engines** can index the front page, the plans list and each plan's page. Nothing about a person, a group, an outing or a crew ever has a public page.

## The front page

- **Headline:** "**Issho means together.** The things you already love, planned for you and a few others." The final wording in each language is read by the readers.
- **The story.** One outing along a gold thread, set in Da Nang–Hoi An (groups of 3 to 6, the hear-by date, no wind-down). The last scene keeps the crew as something that can happen, not a promise.
- **Pictures** are the app's own screens in the app's own wording, drawn from v2's real screens and showing a real vouched plan. No stock photos and no photos of people.
- **The name section** has four characters (一 one, 糸 thread, 者 person, 日 sun/day) and sits at the end of the story, after the crew scene, where the gold thread ends. Its heading is "Look closely: the word tells the whole story."; "means together" is said once, in the headline. Its first line is "Every outing starts with one yes." The Japanese sign-off in the footer stays.
- **The city's real open plans** sit directly under the story.
- **Actions.** "See the plans" is always there. The main action is "Join the waitlist" in the Pilot and the soft launch, and "Sign up" at the public launch. The front page never asks "have a link?"; people with a join link arrive by it.

## Ways in

- **Joining the waitlist is signing in**: email, the 6-digit code, then the city, filled in from the page. There is no separate form, and the code proves the address. The confirmation is the first of the waitlist's three emails.
- **A waitlisted person** sees the plans and one status line: "You're on the waitlist for Da Nang–Hoi An. We'll email you when your place opens." No queue position and no date. They can change their city or display language, or delete the account.
- **A plan's page** shows the whole plan as the app does (stops, meeting point, record, hear-by date) with one button, "Sign up". After sign-in a member picks their times; anyone else joins the waitlist and is told "you'll be able to sign up for this when your place opens". Pasted into a messenger, the link shows a card with the plan's title, the city and its one-line summary.
- **A plan link never breaks.** A closed plan shows as it was, with "not open right now" and no sign-up. A retired plan shows its title, "this plan no longer runs" and the city's open plans, and is dropped from search engines.
- **A join link** opens "⟨member's first name⟩ invited you to Issho in Da Nang–Hoi An", the city's plans, and one action, "Accept". Accepting is signing in there in the browser, which redeems the link; the app is offered afterwards and the person signs in to it with the same email. A used, expired or cancelled link says so and offers the waitlist.
- **Get the app** is one page that sends each device to the right place; a desktop shows a QR code. In the Pilot it is offered only to signed-in members, because test builds can't take strangers. From the soft launch it is public, with store links. The Pilot's printed QR code points at a join link.
- **Cities.** While one city is listed the site is about Da Nang–Hoi An, and a "Not in Da Nang–Hoi An?" line by the waitlist action opens the request form (email, city, country). Once a second city is planned or open, the front page gains a city list, with planned cities marked "opening" and taking a waitlist.

## Other pages

Five and no more, in the city's languages, linked from every page's footer: the code of conduct, the privacy policy, the terms, "delete my account" and "Contact Issho". No blog, no FAQ page, no page recruiting insiders.

## Measuring and reading

- **No trackers** and no cookies beyond sign-in, so no cookie banner. Issho records one thing: the plan or join link a new account arrived by, so the scorecard can show which plans bring people in. It never records which member shared a plan.
- Fonts are served from `joinissho.com`, not from Google ([Privacy](privacy.md)).
- **The site is read like the app**: the site and its five pages are part of what the reader reads before each stage, and nothing on the site is in a language the app isn't.

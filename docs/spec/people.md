# People

From [People](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/552), amended by [Cities](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/555), [Languages](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/556), [Privacy and legal obligations in the product](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/559) and [The v2 glossary and domain model](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/569).

## Sign-in and the account

- **Sign-in** is a 6-digit email code, the only way in for the Pilot. Apple and Google sign-in are added at the soft launch.
- An **account** is an email, a city and a display language, nothing more. It is what a waitlisted person, or a person who only sends a suggestion has. To **join** is to make an account.
- A **profile** is the answers below. It is required at the first signup to a plan (a guest's included) or chat, not before. A person who sends a suggestion accepts the code of conduct without the rest.
- **Adults only.** The full date of birth is asked and the person must be 18 on the day. Issho checks the date and keeps only "said they were 18 or over, on this day".

## Join links

- A member admits a friend with a **join link**: a one-use link the member shares any way they like, redeemed by whichever email the friend signs in with. Issho sends nothing.
- Each member has 3. A link expires after 14 days; an expired or cancelled link returns to the member's 3.
- Issho records who brought in whom; only a moderator sees it ([Safety](safety.md#join-links)).
- "Invitation" is only an outing's offer of a seat.

## Guests

A **guest** is anyone a member adds to their own signup to a plan: a +1.

- **Who can add one.** A member whose standing is ready and who has attended at least one outing. One guest per signup.
- **The link.** The member sends the guest a link any way they like. It shows the plan's card and details with no account needed.
- **Signing up.** On that screen the guest can make an account. They then give their profile and their times and are signed up to the plan like any member.
- **Admission.** A guest who isn't yet a member is admitted by the link, which uses one of the member's 3 join links. A member with none left can add only someone who is already a member.
- **Grouped together.** The member and the guest are treated at once as a mutual pick, so matching puts them in the same group where their times allow ([Matching](matching.md#preferences)). A group is still 3 to 6: the two never make an outing by themselves.
- A guest can't be someone suspended, or kept apart from the member; that link fails without saying why.
- A guest is not a crew matter: nobody brings a guest to a crew outing ([Crews](crews.md)).

## The profile

Asked once, at the first signup, in this order:

1. **The name they go by**: 1 to 40 characters, checked against nothing.
2. **Date of birth.**
3. **Gender**: woman, man, non-binary or prefer not to say; an answer is required, with one line saying why (so nobody is the only woman or the only man in a group). People who answer woman or non-binary are then offered the "only women and non-binary people" setting.
4. **Spoken languages**: "Which languages can you comfortably hold a conversation in for a whole outing?" At least one, any language, no levels ([Languages](languages.md)).
5. **"Do you live in Da Nang–Hoi An?"**: self-declared; "yes" means they are staying three months or more. Either answer is accepted.
6. **The code of conduct.**

The consent ticks that go with the profile are in [Privacy](privacy.md#consent). Nothing is asked about home area, travel, usual availability, tastes, group size, energy or budget. Times are asked at each signup and prefilled from the person's last one.

## Finding plans

Sorting is done on the plans list, not from the profile. Plans are visible before an account exists.

- **Filters**: area, price and activity type. They work without an account and are remembered, on the device before sign-in and on the account after.
- **Area** is one or more of the city's areas that have an open plan, matched against each plan's meeting point. There is no stored home area and no location permission.
- **Price** is "up to ⟨price⟩"; every plan shows its price.
- **Order**: plans the person hasn't done come first and plans they've done sink. Once a city has more than about 30 open plans, activity types they've signed up to and rated 4 or more on "send a friend" rise. Below that, everyone sees one order.
- A plan shows an "N people you got on with signed up" count: a count, mutual picks only.

## What others see

- The group sees the **first name only**: no photo, bio, age, gender or languages.
- A private **"you met at ⟨plan⟩, ⟨month⟩"** marker shows beside anyone the viewer has been on an outing with.
- The group sees the current name; a moderator also sees past names, so a rename doesn't shed a report.

## Residents and cities

- **Residents first, at every stage.** Issho is built for people who live in the city, and nobody is turned away for not living there ([Who Issho is for](who-issho-is-for.md#the-person)).
- A person belongs to **one city at a time**, changed in settings. Their history, standing and kept-apart pairs carry over; their crews stay behind. Switching to a planned city joins its waitlist.
- A visitor with no account sees the city of the link they arrived by, else the only listed city, else picks from a list ([Cities](cities.md#which-city-a-visitor-with-no-account-sees)).

## Languages

Spoken languages and the display language are separate. The profile asks only the spoken ones. A person has one display language: the language they were reading in when they joined, changed in settings and never asked ([Languages](languages.md)).

## What a person can change

Everything on the profile except the date of birth. A gender change is logged for a moderator. The "only women and non-binary people" setting can be switched at any time, applies from the next group formed, and turns off by itself if the person's gender changes to one that isn't eligible.

## The code of conduct

Six rules: be kind and respectful; come with platonic intentions and respect boundaries (feelings aren't banned, unwanted pursuit is reportable); respect privacy; turn up when going and cancel early if you can't, or lose priority and then be paused; meet in the agreed public place and drink responsibly; report anyone who makes you or others uncomfortable.

It is accepted with the profile and the accepted version is stored. A new version blocks anything new until accepted (the standing "needs agreement"), and the person keeps what they are already going to.

## Standing

A person has exactly one **standing**, the most restrictive that applies.

| Standing | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Waitlisted | Has asked to join a city and hasn't been admitted |
| Needs profile | Has an account, hasn't given the profile |
| Needs agreement | Has a new code of conduct or notice to agree to |
| Ready | Can do everything |
| Paused | Timed, from late cancels and no-shows |
| Suspended | Open-ended, for conduct, by a moderator |

A **member** is a person with any standing but waitlisted. **Active** is not a standing: it means signed up to a plan or went on an outing in the last 30 days. A hold ([Safety](safety.md)) is not a standing either.

## Needs and access

The profile asks nothing about accessibility, diet or similar. A plan states its conditions (steps, distance on foot, what the food is) and the person decides at signup.

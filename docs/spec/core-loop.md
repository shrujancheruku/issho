# The core loop

From [The core loop](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/539), amended by [The outing](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/550).

## The loop

1. **See plans.** Anyone can see a city's plans without an account, in the app or from a shared link. A plan has no date and no group, and shows the date by which a person will hear.
2. **Sign up, with times.** The person signs in, and gives their profile at their first signup. A signup commits them to nothing. They can sign up to any number of plans; an outing they are going to blocks that time in their other signups. There is no cap on signups or on outings per week.
3. **Wait, at most 7 days.** They see how many are interested, never who.
4. **Invited.** Matching sets a date and a group of 3 to 6, aiming for 4 or 5. It invites up to two more than it needs, and the outing is set once enough say yes. An invitation is open for a fixed 48 hours.
5. **Going.** This is the one commitment. Cancelling is free until 24 hours before the start. The person sees the first names of their own group only. The meeting point is public on the plan; only the date, time and group are private.
6. **On the day.** They check in. An outing still goes ahead with 2 if people dropped out after it was set; matching never forms a pair on purpose.
7. **After.** Feedback: how it was, who else was there, and who they would meet again.
8. **Again.** Mutual picks make it more likely those people are grouped together. What follows is in [Crews](crews.md).

Plan first, time second: with few people, pooling interest over time matters more than the simplicity of a fixed date. Insiders' plans go round the same loop; an insider never picks the date or the people.

## Where it breaks

- **Too few people.** When a round ends with no group, the people left waiting are told, not moved to another plan. They, and people who have never had an outing, come first when groups are next formed.
- **How many plans a city has.** The number to maximise is the share of people who signed up and got an outing, not the share of plans filled. Many rounds ending with no group is accepted.
- **Late cancels and no-shows.** A late cancel or a no-show counts against the person. The first is forgiven, repeats lower their place in the seat order, and a pattern pauses them. There is no money penalty. The numbers are in [Safety](safety.md#late-cancels-and-no-shows).
- **Claimed attendance.** A person attended if they checked in or said so in feedback, and their own "I didn't go" wins. When the group's word says someone didn't come, that wins over the person's own claim. The rule is in [Outings](outings.md).
- **A bad plan.** A plan that fails its checks isn't opened: a weak plan costs more trust than a missing one. A plan rated poorly does not run again until Issho or its maker has changed it. See [The plan bar](plan-bar.md).

## The north-star number

People whose second outing included someone from an earlier one.

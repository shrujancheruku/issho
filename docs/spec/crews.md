# Crews

From [Crews](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/547), amended by [The v2 glossary and domain model](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/569), with [Chat](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/551) and [Running Issho](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/557).

## What a crew is

A **crew** is a set of 3 to 8 people who have all met and picked each other, whose defining act is a **crew outing**: an outing of its own, on one of the city's plans, with no strangers. Quiet regrouping of mutual picks by matching stays as the step before a crew; a crew is what makes "an easy way to see the same people again" more than chance.

A crew is offered, open, resting or closed. A crew is never called a group.

Picks stay silent. There is no pair feature and no "you two matched" notice: groups are never pairs, and it would read as dating. The crew offer is the first sign of a mutual pick.

## Who is offered one, and how it forms

- **The basis.** A set of at least 3 where everyone has met everyone at some outing (not necessarily the same one), nobody is kept apart from anyone, and each has a mutual pick inside the set.
- **Who starts it.** Issho offers by rule. People never assemble a crew by hand (visible exclusion, pressure). The owner approves each offer until 10 crews in a row have been approved unchanged; approval does not return in a later city, though the owner can switch it back on with a reason.
- **The offer.** Open 7 days; the crew forms with at least 3 accepting; the same set is never offered twice. Declining does not count against anyone: people decline because they're busy or leaving town.
- **More than one crew.** No cap per person. A new crew is not offered if more than half of its crew members come from one existing crew, or if it would contain more than half of an existing crew. A crew of 3 can share one crew member with a new one. Closed crews don't count.

## Crew outings

- **Two routes.** Any crew member can **propose** any of the city's open plans to the crew at any time: every plan's page has "propose to a crew". Otherwise Issho proposes one a week when nothing is in play, favouring plans no crew member has done. One proposal is live at a time.
- **Answering.** Crew members answer yes with times, or no. Who said yes is shown by name, inside the crew only. Once 3 or more overlap, Issho sets the date and it is an outing. A proposal with no quorum after 7 days lapses quietly.
- **Crew-only.** A crew outing holds its crew members plus at most one guest. It does not add the crew members to the plan's signups.
- **Seats.** A crew outing holds 6, like any outing. Seats go in the order crew members said yes, among those whose times fit the date; the rest see "full". Issho never splits one proposal into two outings.
- **Otherwise an ordinary outing.** "Going" is the same commitment with the same late-cancel and no-show costs, check-in and feedback; its answers count towards the plan's record and renew vouches. In feedback, "meet again" is not asked between crew members; "rather not" and reports still apply.
- **Counts, never names** holds without exception outside the crew: a crew member's own signups to plans are invisible to their crew.
- Crew outings don't count in the got-an-outing share ([Matching](matching.md)).

## Guests

- A crew member can **bring a guest** to a crew outing: one guest per outing, only once the date is set and a seat is still free, so a guest never takes a crew member's seat.
- A **guest** can be anyone, including a friend who has never used Issho, but needs an account and a profile before the outing so conduct rules, reporting and feedback apply. A guest can't be suspended or kept apart from any crew member; that invitation fails without saying why.
- Those going are told who is bringing whom; there is no veto beforehand. The guest's invitation says plainly that it is for this outing only and is not an offer of a place in the crew.
- The guest does not count towards the 3 crew members a crew outing needs.

This ticket lets a guest be "anyone" with an account, and no ticket says whether a guest must be a resident or what standing a guest who was never admitted has after the outing. ## How a guest becomes a crew member

- After the outing, each crew member who was there is privately asked "would you want them in the crew?": yes, no or not sure. The crew member who brought them is asked too; bringing them is not counted as a yes.
- Issho offers the guest a place only when every current crew member has met them at some outing, all said yes, and the guest picked at least one of them. A crew member absent that time can be covered by a later outing with the same guest.
- A single "no" ends it silently and for good for that crew. Nobody learns who said it, and the guest never learns there was a question.
- A crew of 8 can't grow.

## Leaving and ending

- **Leaving** is silent, always open and asks why. It makes a flag only when the reason given is "made me uncomfortable" ([Safety](safety.md#flags)). "Silent" means no announcement and no reason shown; in a small crew the others will see who is gone.
- **A quiet crew member.** One who answers no proposal for 8 weeks is privately asked "still in?". No reply in 7 days and they leave silently. They can return only as a guest who is then voted in.
- **Removal.** A suspension, or two crew members becoming kept apart, removes the person reported or not wanted.
- **Below 3.** The crew closes at once, with no grace period. Those who remain stay mutual picks for matching, and a new crew can be offered when a third person fits.
- **Inactivity.** After 8 weeks without an outing the crew is **resting**: Issho's proposals stop, and any crew member's proposal wakes it. After 4 more weeks with no proposal, Issho privately asks each crew member "keep this crew?". Seven days later, with fewer than 3 yeses it closes; with 3 or more it carries on with those who said yes, and the others leave silently.
- Crew members of a closed crew are told "this crew has closed", with no reasons.
- When a person changes city, their crews stay behind ([People](people.md)). When a city closes, its crews end ([Cities](cities.md)).

## The room

A crew has one room, open as long as the crew is. The rules, and when a crew outing gets a room of its own, are in [Chat](chat.md).

## The name

A crew shows as its crew members' first names by default. When it forms, a vote opens for 3 days: 3 generated names in a language the crew shares (avoiding gang, clan and squad connotations), plus any the crew members add. Each picks one; most picks wins, a tie goes to the earliest name put forward, and with no votes it stays as first names. Any crew member can reopen the vote, at most once every 30 days. The name is seen only by crew members and guests, is shown as written, and a name put forward can be reported like a message.

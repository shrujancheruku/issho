# Who Issho is for and what it promises

From [Who Issho is for and what it promises](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/533), amended by [Building blocks](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/544) and [People](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/552).

## The person

People who live in the city or are staying long enough to want people to do things with there, local and foreign alike.

**Residents first** is a guiding principle: every decision is made for people who live in the city. Nobody is turned away. Someone passing through who wants to join is welcome, and nothing is designed for them.

## The problem

"I have no group to do things with here, and the ways to meet people are one-off events where I never see anyone again." The outing is the means; seeing the same people again is the end.

## Why small groups

Everyone can talk to everyone, so nobody is a spectator. Sizes are in [The core loop](core-loop.md).

## Languages

Something Issho handles, not something it sells. Every group shares a language all of its people speak, locals and foreigners mix where that allows, and the app works in each person's display language. The mix is a welcome effect, not a promise. Detail is in [Languages](languages.md).

## The promise

An outing worth going to with a few people you can talk to, planned for you; and if you got on, an easy way to see the same people again. Success for a person is a second outing with at least one person from the first. Issho does not promise friends.

## Who makes plans

Issho, by default. Insiders, by invitation, make and look after their own plans in their city. Making plans is never open to ordinary members. Detail is in [Plans](plans.md).

An insider has no duty to attend and no special place in a group they join. A guide on a guided activity belongs to the activity and is not an insider.

## What Issho is not

Not a dating product, an events listing, a host marketplace or a social network with a feed. It is a general planner of activities; a language exchange or a tour can be an activity, but the product and its architecture are built around neither.

## Money, in principle

The people who go on outings are the ones who pay. Insiders may earn from their plans. Venues never pay for placement in plans. The model is in [The stages of launch](launch.md).

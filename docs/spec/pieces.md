# Pieces

From [Building blocks](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/544), with answers from [Routes worth walking](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/546) and [Cities](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/issho/issues/555).

## What a piece is

- A **piece** is a vouched thing a plan can be made from. The vouch, the facts recorded with it (cost per head, whether it takes six, booking, real hours, the months it runs, whether it needs dry weather or daylight) and its re-check clocks live on the piece. Plans refer to pieces; a vouch is made once and every plan using the piece inherits it.
- A piece is vouched, doubted or retired.
- The plan is still reviewed against the bar as a whole: good pieces don't make a good plan by themselves.

## Piece types

| Piece type | What it is | How it is vouched |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Activity** | Something concrete the group does together | In person |
| **Food stop** | A café, stall, restaurant or bar | Two independent local sources, then the first group (see the unresolved note in [Plan knowledge](plan-knowledge.md#vouching)) |
| **Meeting point** | A signed, one-of-a-kind place | In person, by someone who has stood there |
| **Stretch** | A way on foot someone has walked and found pleasant | In person, walked end to end ([Routes](routes.md#vouching-a-stretch)) |

- A park is not a piece type: it is a stretch if the group walks through it, or the setting of an activity. A thing of interest along a walk (a mural, a viewpoint) is a note on a stretch.
- **An activity is concrete**: one specific thing to do at a specific place or with a specific provider (the Saturday wheel class at a named studio), because that is the only thing a person can visit and vouch for. There is no abstract activity catalogue.
- **Each use of a place is its own piece with its own vouch.** A café vouched for its food is not a meeting point until someone has stood there. Pieces at one **place** share its identity (the Google place ID), so a "permanently closed" flag doubts all of them at once. A stretch has no place ID.
- Every piece belongs to one city and to one of that city's areas, picked by the person who vouches ([Cities](cities.md)).

## Whose they are, and who sees them

- A piece belongs to Issho from the moment it is vouched. Any maker (an insider in that city, the owner, a model drafting from vouched knowledge) can use any piece, and it stays when the person who vouched leaves.
- If the person who vouched is suspended for conduct, their pieces are treated as doubted until re-checked.
- Members never see a piece on its own. They see plans, and a piece appears only as a **stop** in one. Issho is not a places directory, and there is no placement for a venue to want.

## Plans from pieces

- **Every place a plan names is a piece.** A plan has at least one meeting point and one activity. Food stops and stretches are optional support. Free text (title, description, notes, what to bring) names no place that isn't a piece.
- The plan adds the order, the time at each stop, the legs, the notes and its conditions. Its cost is the sum of its pieces' costs, and the days, hours and months it can run are those when all its pieces are open.
- **One activity, one plan.** An activity belongs to a single plan; a second plan around the same activity can exist only once the first is retired. A maker who has a better idea for the activity edits its plan. Other pieces can be in any number of plans.
- **Food is an ordinary stop**, named when the plan opens and counted in its cost and length. A plan may have no food. There is no wind-down.

## Losing standing

- A **doubted** piece, or one past its re-check date, fails the accuracy check for every plan that uses it. None opens a new round until the piece is re-checked or swapped. Rounds with people already signed up or going get accuracy fixes only, and those people are told.
- A **like-for-like swap** replaces a food stop, meeting point or stretch with another vouched piece of the same piece type that fits the same slot (near enough, open then, similar cost). It needs no review and the plan keeps its record. Swapping the activity is never like-for-like: it makes a new plan with a new record.
- A piece is **retired** when a re-check finds it no longer good or no longer suits a group, when Google's closed flag is confirmed, or when it has been doubted for 14 days without a re-check. A retired piece stays on record with the reason, so the same lead isn't vouched again blind.
- Any insider in the city, or the owner, can re-check or retire a piece, not only whoever vouched for it.
- Ratings stay on the plan; people are never asked to rate a stop. A piece that appears in two different plans rated poorly is sent for a re-check, as a signal only.

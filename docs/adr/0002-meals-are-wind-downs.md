# Meals and beverages are wind-downs, not activities

A meal used to be an activity like any other: "Lunch or dinner out" and "Brunch or breakfast" were in the catalogue, people picked them as interests, matching paired them as a follow-up, and their venue was chosen when the plan was created, before anyone knew whether the outing would fall at lunch or dinner. We decided (2026-11-05, #347) that food is the social glue around an outing rather than its point: a meal or a beverage (a café, a tea house) is an optional **wind-down** after the activities, chosen once the date is set by the time it starts and a venue nearby that's open then, and a beverage place can also be a city's meeting place. Neither is something people sign up for, check in to or give feedback on, so a person who'd rather go home after bouldering can.

The alternative was to keep meals as activities and only reword "Lunch or dinner out" once the date is known. It was smaller, but it kept meals in matching, where they don't belong (nobody should be left out of a plan for not liking dinners), and it couldn't place a meal near where the activity ends. Outings that are about food or drink (a food tour, a cooking class, a barbecue, drinks out, a café walk) stay activities and get no wind-down.
